Grade 8: Home Anchor Evidence

DESIGN CLASS G8TH / 2026-2027

Unit Title: Can an object be playful, even if it is not meant for playing with?

Key Concepts: Identity, Form | Related Concepts: Culture, Empathy

Global Context: Personal and Cultural Expression (Exploration: Ritual and Play)

Statement of Inquiry: The form of a designed object can communicate identity and cultural connection, transforming a simple toy into a meaningful anchor for someone experiencing change or uncertainty.

Session 1: Oct 4, 2026 - Introduction to Home Anchor

Classroom Activity

Students were introduced to the unit and the concept of "Home Anchor". We discussed what reminds us of home, sharing personal experiences of leaving home (migration/war contexts). Students brainstormed and wrote down their own "Home Concepts" on a sheet.

Assignment

Create a "Home Concept Collage" using the provided structure to visually represent their ideas.

IB Connection, ATL & Command Terms

ATL Focus: Self-Management (Organization) & Creative Thinking.

Evidence: Students practiced organization and empathy by reflecting on personal memories and structuring their ideas into a concept for their collage.

Command Terms:

Identify Brainstorm

Session 2: Oct 11, 2026 - Collages & Japanese Art Toys

Classroom Activity

Students presented their Home Concept collages. We then explored Japanese Art Toys (Kaiju, Sofubi, etc.) and famous designers from the textbook.

Assignment

Choose a designer, a specific toy, or a toy company. Complete one of the provided Google Slides templates (Analyzing Existing Products) to research their style.

IB Connection, ATL & Command Terms

ATL Focus: Research (Information Literacy) & Communication.

Evidence: Students demonstrated research skills by locating information about specific designers and analyzing their visual style using the provided Google Slides template.

Command Terms:

Describe Analyse

Session 3: Oct 18, 2026 - Persona & Empathy Map

Classroom Activity

We introduced the concept of "Persona". In groups, students categorized character cards using their own rules. We watched a video on Persona and discussed "Jobs-to-be-Done". Finally, students conducted pair interviews based on their Home Concept using an Empathy Map.

Assignment

No formal homework, but students prepared for the next step of their design journey based on the Empathy Map data.

IB Connection, ATL & Command Terms

ATL Focus: Collaboration & Communication.

Evidence: Students practiced active listening and empathy by interviewing their peers about their "Home Concept" and recording their thoughts on an Empathy Map.

Command Terms:

Classify Investigate

Session 4: Oct 25, 2026 - Deep Dive into Persona

Classroom Activity

Students watched more detailed videos on Persona and Empathy Maps. They then created their own Persona profiles (representing themselves) based on the Empathy Maps they completed the previous week.

Assignment

Complete the written characteristics of their Persona based on the Empathy Map.

IB Connection, ATL & Command Terms

ATL Focus: Self-Management (Reflection) & Critical Thinking.

Evidence: Students demonstrated reflection by analyzing their own "Home Concept" to create a detailed Persona profile that captures their emotional needs and identity.

Command Terms:

Identify Explain

Session 5: Nov 1, 2026 - Initial Sketches & Design Brief

Classroom Activity

Based on their Persona and Empathy Map, students created initial sketches of their Art Toy. We watched a video explaining the "Design Brief".

Assignment

Write a Design Brief and Persona. Finalize the Home Concept and Art Style (from chosen designer). Create a poster featuring the initial sketch and an AI-improved sketch of the toy.

IB Connection, ATL & Command Terms

ATL Focus: Creative Thinking & Self-Management (Organization).

Evidence: Students applied creative thinking to sketch their ideas and used organization skills to compile their research, Persona, and sketches into a comprehensive poster presentation.

Command Terms:

Design Create

Session 6: Nov 8, 2026 - Refining Ideas & NFC Planning

Classroom Activity

Students continued improving their sketches. We discussed how to integrate QR codes or NFC tags into the final Art Toy. Initial explanations of NFC technology and production planning were provided.

Assignment

Finalize the production plan. Prepare for the physical making of the Art Toy, where the NFC integration will be taught in detail.

IB Connection, ATL & Command Terms

ATL Focus: Self-Management (Time & Resources) & Transfer.

Evidence: Students managed their resources and time by planning the physical creation of their toy. They also began to understand how to transfer their digital ideas into a physical object using emerging technology (NFC).

Command Terms:

Develop Plan
